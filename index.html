Auto-Pay Disclaimer

Hindi: Auto pay set karne par, "One Time" waale ke liye jis date/time par set kiya hai, usi date aur time par aapke bank account se automatically paise cut jaayenge. "Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly/Alternative" waale ke liye har baar order lagega aur usi tay time par payment automatic cut jayegi.

English: Once auto-pay is set, on a One Time schedule, the payment will be deducted automatically from your bank account on the selected date and time. For recurring schedules (Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly/Alternative), an order will be placed and payment auto-deducted each time at the set time — all automatically.

Hinglish: Auto pay set karne ke baad ye pura process automatic ho jayega — aapko baar baar order lagane ki zaroorat nahi. Aap kabhi bhi "Temporary Off" karke ise pause kar sakte ho.
